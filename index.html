Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the OpenUtility platform

Are my uploaded files or data saved on a server?

No. All image manipulation and client-side utilities execute entirely within your browser using JavaScript HTML5 Canvas APIs. Your images and inputs never leave your device or get uploaded to any external server.

Is OpenUtility completely free?

Yes, 100% free and open-source. There are no paywalls, subscriptions, rate limits, or advertisements.

How can I contribute or report bugs?

You can open an issue or pull request directly on our GitHub repository. We welcome community suggestions and contribution to new web utilities.
